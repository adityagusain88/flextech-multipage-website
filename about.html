WHO WE ARE

Practical engineering for
manufacturing needs.

Flextech Automation Technologies provides both fixture design and development, as well as manufacturing of precision fixtures for the automobile industry.

Customers can approach us with an existing engineering drawing or with a requirement that needs to be developed into a fixture solution. We work from customer-provided drawings and specifications, and can also support fixture concept development around the component, process and agreed quality requirements. Our focus includes welding fixtures and checking fixtures for two-wheeler and four-wheeler applications, alongside inspection, assembly and customised fixture requirements.

We believe effective fixtures should support accurate positioning, consistent inspection and repeatable manufacturing operations.
