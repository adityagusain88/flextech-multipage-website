# Flextech Automation Technologies — Multi-page website

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — About Us
- `solutions.html` — Welding, checking, inspection and assembly fixtures
- `industries.html` — Automotive industry applications
- `capabilities.html` — Engineering approach and capabilities
- `gallery.html` — Illustrative gallery
- `contact.html` — Contact details and enquiry form design preview

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or run a local static server:
`python -m http.server 8000`

## Deploy to Vercel
1. Replace the files in your existing GitHub repository with the contents of this folder.
2. Commit and push to your connected branch.
3. Vercel will automatically redeploy. Framework preset: Other; build command: blank; output directory: `.`.

## Before public launch
- Replace illustrative Unsplash photos with actual Flextech project and facility photographs.
- Confirm the official business email, phone number and full address.
- The contact form is intentionally a visual preview and is not connected to email delivery yet. Email integration is a separate next step.
- No certifications, customer names, machinery, capacity or years of experience have been claimed.


## Flextech engagement model
Website copy explains both service routes:
1. Manufacture fixtures from customer-provided drawings and specifications.
2. Support fixture design and development based on the customer's component, process and quality requirements.

Specific software, machinery, certifications and technical capacity are not claimed unless confirmed by the company.
